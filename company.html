COMPANY

Building a general-purpose robot brain
for the real world.

Riemann Dynamics develops embodied intelligence grounded in world models. Incubated by the team behind Matrix-Game, we are building Riemann-1.0, our world-action model, to connect world understanding, future prediction, and action generation—enabling robots to perform complex tasks in the real world.

We bring models, robotic hardware, and data together in an integrated development loop. Built on over 230,000 hours of high-quality embodied data and our in-house direct-drive dexterous hand, this approach connects tactile sensing, data collection, and model learning. We focus on home, eldercare, and healthcare applications, making our capabilities accessible through Model-as-a-Service (MaaS) and APIs.
