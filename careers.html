Electronics Hardware Engineer — Dexterous Hands

Responsibilities

  • Design electrical systems for dexterous hands, covering power, communications, sensing, and protection circuits.
  • Develop control, power, and motor drive boards from component selection and PCB design through bring-up and debugging.
  • Integrate hardware across joints and fingers; support prototype validation, supplier coordination, and product delivery.

Requirements

  • A relevant bachelor’s degree or higher and 3+ years of hardware development experience; exceptions may be considered for candidates with outstanding project track records.
  • Experience taking circuit boards from design through system integration, with proficiency in circuit design, PCB layout, and EDA tools.
  • Working knowledge of motor drive hardware, sensor interfaces, and communication buses, plus strong hands-on debugging skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with dexterous hands, robotic joints, compact PCBs, tactile sensing, or EMC and reliability validation.

Motor Development Engineer — Dexterous Hands

Responsibilities

  • Design miniature BLDC/PMSM motors for dexterous hands, optimizing torque density, efficiency, and torque ripple.
  • Run electromagnetic and thermal simulations; support prototyping, supplier coordination, and joint-level integration.
  • Establish motor test suites and parameter identification workflows to validate performance and guide design iterations.

Requirements

  • A relevant bachelor’s degree or higher and 3+ years of motor development experience; exceptions may be considered for candidates with outstanding project track records.
  • End-to-end motor development experience, from electromagnetic design to prototype validation.
  • Proficiency in tools such as Ansys Maxwell, JMAG, or Motor-CAD; understanding of manufacturing processes, thermal limits, and FOC fundamentals.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with miniature direct-drive, frameless, coreless, or slotless motors, or precision motor characterization and testing.

Motor Drive and Embedded Systems Engineer — Dexterous Hands

Responsibilities

  • Develop BLDC/PMSM drive firmware and high-precision current, velocity, position, and torque control loops.
  • Implement real-time embedded control for 15–25 joints, including communication, synchronization, diagnostics, and firmware update mechanisms.
  • Integrate motors and control subsystems; validate torque accuracy, control bandwidth, thermal performance, and long-term reliability.

Requirements

  • A relevant bachelor’s degree or higher and 3+ years of motor drive, servo control, or embedded development experience; exceptions may be considered for candidates with outstanding project track records.
  • Proficiency in C/C++, MCU development, and real-time operating systems, with hands-on FOC and servo control implementation experience.
  • Experience with deterministic multi-joint control, fieldbus protocols, and hardware/software co-debugging; understanding of force and impedance control.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with dexterous hands, distributed control architectures, EtherCAT/CAN-FD, torque calibration, or friction compensation.
